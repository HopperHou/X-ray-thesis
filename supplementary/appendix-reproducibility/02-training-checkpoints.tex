\section{Checkpoints, Environment and Seed-level Results}
\label{app:checkpoint-environment}

Rasters loaded as three channels or from cache, underwent longer-side resizing to 1024, and were affinely mapped to $1024\times1024$. Uncovered pixels were set to 114 before photometric augmentation. Boxes followed resizing, affine transformation, flipping and clipping; floating intensities were divided by 255 before network input. Seed 42 was prespecified as representative, with all-epoch losses retained.



Historical runs used PyTorch 2.11.0+cu126, CUDA 12.6 and Ultralytics 8.4.83, with 30/40/eight loader workers for M3/E50/evaluation. Tables~\ref{tab:validation-main} and~\ref{tab:app-per-seed-results} report aggregate and per-seed historical results.

Table~\ref{tab:training-stages} brings together detector adaptation, Q-only preparation and matched fine-tuning. A0-R was selected by validation mAP50--95, M3 duration by inner grouped-training Q loss, and final comparisons used fixed E50 checkpoints.
