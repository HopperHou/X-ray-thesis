\section{Dataset Curation and Reference Construction}
\label{app:data-curation}

\subsection{VinDr-CXR record history and geometry measurements}

Replaying verified same-class enclosing-box consolidation produced 11,339 training-branch boxes, compared with 11,296 in the later source table. Twelve missing image identifiers accounted for 34 boxes, and nine additional two-to-one envelopes accounted for the remaining nine. In consensus data, removing one \textit{Aortic enlargement} box and consolidating two \textit{Cardiomegaly} boxes reduced 1,154 targets to 1,152. The decision rules for those later changes were not recovered. The frozen annotation files preserve the reference actually used in the experiments.

Geometry measurements covered 3,009 verified consolidation groups from 2,212 image identifiers after reader fusion and target selection. Each group's largest-area input box supplied the reference, with CSV row order breaking ties. For output/reference subscripts $o/r$, relative width and height changes were $(w_o-w_r)/w_r$ and $(h_o-h_r)/h_r$. Area and aspect-ratio ratios were $w_oh_o/(w_rh_r)$ and $(w_o/h_o)/(w_r/h_r)$, with aspect ratio defined as width divided by height. Medians and linearly interpolated quartiles described the verified consolidation stage, excluding preceding WBF and later record changes.

\subsection{RSNA--ChestX-Det duplicate confirmation and consolidation}

Decoded-grayscale SHA-256 screened for exact image matches. Perceptual retrieval and full-resolution comparison used 0.5th/99.5th-percentile intensity clipping followed by linear rescaling to 8-bit $[0,255]$. Concatenated $16\times16$ aHash/dHash descriptors supplied 512-bit representations. Each ChestX-Det image's five nearest RSNA neighbours by Hamming distance underwent full-resolution verification. Verification selected the higher pixel-wise Pearson correlation between direct and intensity-inverted grayscale orientations. For $N$ pixels, standardised mean absolute error was
\[
\operatorname{zMAE}(I,J)=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{p=1}^{N}\left|\frac{I_p-\mu_I}{\sigma_I}-\frac{J_p-\mu_J}{\sigma_J}\right|.
\]
Pairs qualified through exact decoded-array agreement or selected correlation $\geq0.995$ together with zMAE $\leq0.10$. No exact hashes matched. All 123 confirmed one-to-one pairs had correlation $>0.9995$.

Joint records retained the RSNA raster and UUID, source RSNA/ChestX-Det and a mapping to both original identifiers. Their common $1024\times1024$ coordinates required no rescaling. All positive boxes remained without averaging or deduplication. Conflicting negative rows were removed, and paired negatives became one record. The 94 positive/positive, 15 mixed and 14 negative/negative pairs yielded 109 positive and 14 negative records. Removing 29 negative rows reduced annotation rows from 31,153 to 31,124, while image-record consolidation reduced 18,561 records to 18,438.

\subsection{Coordinate validation and raster provenance}

All sources used original dimensions $W/H$ and pixel corners satisfying $0\leq x_{\min}<x_{\max}\leq W$ and $0\leq y_{\min}<y_{\max}\leq H$. Raster dimensions were verified. Missing positive coordinates and out-of-bounds or degenerate boxes failed validation after source corrections. Task-negative records had empty coordinates and empty YOLO label files. Valid boxes became normalised centres and dimensions through
\[
\left(\frac{x_{\min}+x_{\max}}{2W},\frac{y_{\min}+y_{\max}}{2H},\frac{x_{\max}-x_{\min}}{W},\frac{y_{\max}-y_{\min}}{H}\right).
\]
Existing MS-CXR/VinDr-CXR JPG and RSNA/ChestX-Det/SIIM-ACR PNG rasters were used without re-encoding. The recovered SIIM-ACR conversion applied DICOM slope/intercept, MONOCHROME1 correction and 0.5th--99.5th-percentile linear 8-bit mapping. Collapsed percentiles triggered full-range mapping. Other earlier conversion histories were incomplete.

\subsection{Patient linkage, partitioning and retained repeats}

MS-CXR linkage used MIMIC path subjects. RSNA and confirmed ChestX-Det/SIIM-ACR origins shared a NIH patient namespace, while 13 unresolved SIIM-ACR origins used local DICOM PatientIDs. Origin matching supported linkage without globally consolidating repeated images. Patient identifiers grouped 12,664 records, and the 5,774 VinDr-CXR records without patient identifiers remained singletons.

Seed-42 deterministic greedy multilabel stratification kept groups intact across 14,934 training, 1,660 validation and 1,844 internal-test records. Stratification balanced source, positive/negative status, image-level classes and box counts. The subsequent audit identified 357 retained repeated-image groups, comprising 355 cross-source groups involving SIIM-ACR and two within-source pairs. All remained within partitions, with 288 training, 35 validation and 34 internal-test groups. The audit established no cross-partition repeat among the identified groups, while global image uniqueness and complete patient separation remained unverified.

\subsection{Frozen reference files}

The frozen \path{All_data.csv} records positive boxes and task-negative images with identifiers, source, labels, dimensions and pixel corners. Final annotations are preserved in \path{new_train.csv}, \path{new_validation.csv} and \path{new_test.csv}. Group assignments are stored in \path{patient_level_split_image_assignments.csv}, and \path{dataset_manifest.csv} links image and label files. Recoverable scripts and records are available at \mbox{\href{https://github.com/HopperHou/X-ray-thesis/tree/main/dataset}{Dataset and preprocessing records (GitHub)}}.
